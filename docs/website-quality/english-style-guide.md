# English style guide

Audience: international B2B buyers and partners. Tone: direct, specific, calm.

## Rules
- British spelling, consistently (modelling, organise, enquiry). Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Discuss your project", not "Discuss Your Project").
- One claim per sentence; concrete nouns; active voice ("We review every change", not "Every change is ensured to be reviewed").
- No unsupported superlatives: best-in-class, world-leading, cutting-edge, revolutionary, seamless.
- Company voice: "we" / "Brisasoft". First person singular only in the founder message.
- Keep employee categories distinct: *full-time employees* ≠ *engineers* ≠ *partner engineers* ≠ *team members*.

## Formats
| Item | Format |
|---|---|
| Dates | October 2024 · as of October 2026 |
| Headcount | 12 full-time employees (as of October 2026) |
| Money | Approx. JPY 7 million equivalent |
| Phone | +84 927 997 699 |
| Founding | Established in 2024 / founded in October 2024 |
| Ratios | 70 / 30 with its definition next to it |

## Terminology
AI-native engineering · AI application development · business process automation · business systems and integration · infrastructure and operations · knowledge assistant (RAG) · AI agent · Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) · working prototype · in development.

## Primary call to action
"Discuss your project" everywhere (opens an email with a pre-filled subject). Secondary links describe their destination ("See our products", "View our services").
